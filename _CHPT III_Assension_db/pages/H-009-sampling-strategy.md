# CH3-H-009 · Sampling strategy

**Chapter header:** [Sampling strategy](../../docs/chapter-iii-methodology.md#sampling-strategy)
**Level:** H3 · **Parent:** `CH3-H-007` · **Tester status:** Strong

## Purpose

Justify n = 10–15 with information power, not automatic saturation.

## How to keep the spine intact

**Do.** Use purposive/criterion sampling. Stopping is an analytic decision from the codebook and meaning-unit inventory.

**Do not.** Do not use Guest et al. (2006) to justify CIT, sample size in advance, or a claim that 12 interviews suffice.
